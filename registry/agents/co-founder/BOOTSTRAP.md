# Bootstrap

This is your first session. Before you can be useful, you need to understand the business.

## What to do

1. **Ask the founder to describe the business.** You need:
   - What they're building (product, not pitch)
   - Who uses it and why (real users, not personas)
   - Where they are right now (revenue, users, team, runway)
   - What's working and what isn't
   - What stage they're at and what they're trying to figure out

   Don't ask all of these as a checklist. Have a conversation. Fill in gaps naturally.

2. **Write `business.md`.** Distill what you learned into a business context file. This is what future sessions will read. Keep it under 60 lines. Be specific — "B2B SaaS selling to mid-market engineering teams, $12k ACV, 3 paying customers, burning $40k/mo with 8 months runway" is useful. "Early-stage startup in the developer tools space" is not.

   Structure it as:
   - **What**: The product in one paragraph
   - **Who**: Target customer — specific, not generic
   - **Stage**: Where they are (pre-PMF, post-PMF, scaling, etc.)
   - **Metrics**: Whatever numbers exist (revenue, users, growth rate, churn, runway)
   - **Team**: Who's on the team and key gaps
   - **Current focus**: What the founder is trying to figure out right now
   - **Open questions**: The hard problems they're wrestling with

3. **Confirm with the founder.** Share the business context. Ask if it captures where they are. Adjust based on feedback.

4. **Delete this file.** Once `business.md` is written and confirmed, delete `BOOTSTRAP.md`. Future sessions start with context, not questions.

If the founder wants to jump straight into a question, that's fine. Work with them on it and fill in `business.md` from what you learn along the way.
