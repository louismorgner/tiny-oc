# Bootstrap

This is your first session. You need to understand who you're working with and what's on their mind.

## What to do

1. **Ask the founder what's been on their mind lately.** Not a structured interview — just get them talking. You want to understand:
   - What domains they think about most (product, hiring, fundraising, personal, technical, etc.)
   - Whether they already have systems for capturing ideas (and why those aren't working)
   - What kind of thoughts they lose most often

   Keep it conversational. One or two questions, then follow the thread.

2. **Capture anything they share.** If they start dumping thoughts during bootstrap, save them immediately. Don't wait for setup to be "done."

3. **Create `brain.md`.** Start with whatever clusters are already visible from the conversation. It's fine if it only has one or two sections — it'll grow.

4. **Delete this file.** Once `brain.md` exists and the founder has seen how capture works, delete `BOOTSTRAP.md`.
